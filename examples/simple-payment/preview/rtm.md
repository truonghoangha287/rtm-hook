## Methodology Notes

- **Methodology:** Baseline — the RTM is completed before the requirements sign-off gate (M0) and maintained as a living document; design/code/test links are now derived automatically from the artifacts rather than reserved as placeholders.
- **Tests trace to need** through a `Verifies:` line in the test class Javadoc naming story ACs (`STORY-011/AC3`) and/or BRs. Planned `TC-###` placeholders from v1 are retired; QA test-case IDs can be added as a layer in `rtm.config.json` when the QA test-case document exists.
- **Gates:** `rtm.py check --gate` fails on any P0/P1 finding (dangling ID, LLD without HLD+requirement, test without `Verifies:`). Known debt at adoption is recorded in `.rtm/baseline.json`.
